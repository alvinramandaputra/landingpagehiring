About KYZN

A family & social wellness club

KYZN brings sports, wellness, lifestyle and family experiences together under one roof — with programs designed for every member of the family, from toddlers to seniors.

Founded in 2021 at Quantis Clubhouse BSD, KYZN has grown into a network of clubs across Greater Jakarta, Surabaya and Bekasi, guided by one simple idea: continuous, positive change — the Kaizen philosophy — practiced together, every day.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I apply?
Find a position that fits you in the Open Positions section above and click Apply Now. You'll be directed to our application form to submit your details.
Where are KYZN's locations?
KYZN currently operates in Quantis BSD, Hiera BSD, Grand Wisata Bekasi, Surabaya, and Jakarta Selatan – Kuningan (Head Office). See the Locations section for details on each club.
Can I apply for more than one position?
Yes. If you're a fit for more than one opening, you're welcome to submit an application for each position you're interested in.
What happens after I apply?
Our recruitment team reviews every application. If your profile matches an open role, we'll reach out to move forward with screening and next steps.
Can I apply for another position if I don't see a suitable opening?
Our current openings are listed above and updated regularly. Check back to see new positions as they become available.
